Project Atlas started in 2024. It uses semantic circuits to represent knowledge. The team reports that Atlas can reconstruct evidence for its answers. The architecture stores each extracted claim as an executable SOP circuit. Because the answer circuits retain derivation links, the architecture is auditable. Atlas may reduce repeated retrieval work. In this document, a knowledge circuit means an executable SOP circuit generated from source material. The design separates persistent knowledge circuits from temporary Datalog materialization.
